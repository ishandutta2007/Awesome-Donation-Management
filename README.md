# Awesome-Donation-Management

# Top Donation Management Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Online Giving, Donor CRM & Nonprofit Fundraising Operations*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Donation Management**. These tools help nonprofits, churches, and charitable organizations accept online donations, manage donor relationships, process recurring gifts, and generate giving statements.

**Examples** include Givebutter, Donorbox, GiveWP, Qgiv, Network for Good, Classy, Fundraise Up, Funraise, Zeffy, OneCause, Tithe.ly, Pushpay, Givelify, Kindrid, EasyTithe, Vanco Give, Subsplash Giving, Planning Center Giving, and SecureGive (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosting, custom giving workflows, and transparent donor data management — ideal for churches, nonprofits, and developers building vendor-independent donation platforms. The open-source ecosystem is anchored by **CiviCRM** (comprehensive nonprofit CRM), **ChurchCRM** (church management), and **GiveWP** (WordPress donation plugin), with strong coverage in church-specific treasury systems and crowdfunding platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Givebutter](https://givebutter.com/)**  
  Free fundraising platform with donation forms, event ticketing, peer-to-peer fundraising, and donor CRM. 100% free with optional donor tips. Best for online fundraising campaigns and event ticketing . Features personalized contact profiles with transaction history, automatic activity tracking, custom filters, giving summary charts, wealth screening, and custom fields .

- **[Donorbox](https://donorbox.org/)**  
  Free donation forms and recurring giving setup. Small platform fee but free to start. Best for collecting donations on your website .

- **[GiveWP](https://givewp.com/)**  
  WordPress donation plugin with 0% platform fee and full data ownership. Native WordPress integration with recurring donation add-on. Best for organizations with self-hosted WordPress sites wanting complete control .

- **[Qgiv](https://www.qgiv.com/)**  
  Hosted SaaS fundraising platform with 3.95% + $0.30 transaction fees. Comprehensive donation management and event fundraising . Now part of Bloomerang Fundraising .

- **[Network for Good](https://www.networkforgood.com/)**  
  All-in-one SaaS platform with 3% platform fee. Donor management, fundraising, and communications for nonprofits .

- **[Classy](https://www.classy.org/)**  
  Enterprise SaaS platform (now GoFundMe Pro) with variable pricing. Peer-to-peer fundraising and event management .

- **[Fundraise Up](https://fundraiseup.com/)**  
  AI-powered donation platform with optimized checkout, recurring giving, and donor management.

- **[Funraise](https://funraise.org/)**  
  Modern fundraising platform with donation processing, peer-to-peer campaigns, and donor CRM.

- **[Zeffy](https://www.zeffy.com/)**  
  Free for nonprofits with tip-based model (donor tips pre-checked by default). 0% platform fee plus donor tip. Hosted SaaS platform .

- **[OneCause](https://www.onecause.com/)**  
  Fundraising software for events, auctions, and peer-to-peer campaigns.

- **[Tithe.ly](https://tithe.ly/)**  
  Church giving and management platform with online donations, text giving, and church apps.

- **[Pushpay](https://pushpay.com/)**  
  Digital giving platform for churches with mobile app and engagement tools.

- **[Givelify](https://www.givelify.com/)**  
  Mobile giving app for churches and nonprofits.

- **[Kindrid](https://kindrid.com/)**  
  Smart giving platform for churches with text-to-give and online donations.

- **[EasyTithe](https://easytithe.com/)**  
  Online giving solution for churches with recurring donations and reporting.

- **[Vanco Give](https://www.vancopayments.com/)**  
  Electronic giving solutions for churches and nonprofits.

- **[Subsplash Giving](https://www.subsplash.com/)**  
  Church giving and engagement platform integrated with Subsplash apps.

- **[Planning Center Giving](https://www.planningcenter.com/giving)**  
  Church giving platform integrated with Planning Center church management suite.

- **[SecureGive](https://securegive.com/)**  
  Church giving kiosks, online, and mobile giving solutions.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  
  The leading open-source CRM built specifically for nonprofits and mission-driven organizations, used by thousands of organizations worldwide since 2005 . Comprehensive donor lifecycle management, contributions (CiviContribute), events (CiviEvent), memberships (CiviMember), grants (CiviGrant), campaigns (CiviCampaign), bulk communications (CiviMail), and reporting (CiviReport) . AGPL-3.0 licensed with integrations for Stripe, PayPal, QuickBooks, Mailchimp, WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla . **The de facto open-source alternative to Salesforce, Raiser's Edge, and Microsoft Dynamics for nonprofits** . Translated into 30+ languages with thousands of active installations .

- **[ChurchCRM](https://github.com/ChurchCRM/CRM)**  
  Open-source church management system with 40+ language localizations . Helps congregations manage members, track contributions, and streamline communication. Active development with 10,459 commits as of July 2025 . Features member management, contribution tracking, event management, and communication tools. **The most widely adopted open-source church management platform**.

- **[ChurchApps GivingApi](https://github.com/ChurchApps/GivingApi)**  
  Free open-source donation tracking API for churches, part of the ChurchApps suite of free open-source solutions for churches . Actively maintained with recent commits.

- **[Rock RMS](https://github.com/SparkDevNetwork/Rock)**  
  Open-source church management system praised for its customization capabilities and active community . Users describe it as "a set of Legos that comes pre-assembled, functional out of the box, but allows you to fully customize what you see, where you see it, and even how it looks" . Features member management, contribution tracking, attendance, and web integration via WordPress plugin. Free for smaller churches with community support via Slack .

- **[PCEA Church Management System](https://github.com/imutwiri/PCEA-Church-Management-System)**  
  Modern web-based dashboard for church operations with member management, attendance tracking via QR codes, finance management (tithes, offerings, pledges) with MPESA integration, event scheduling, and multi-language support (Swahili/English) .

- **[Sistema de Tesouraria](https://github.com/3jos3a/sistema-tesouraria)**  
  PHP-based church financial management system focused on usability and security . Features worship service management, tithe and offering tracking, expense control, financial reporting, digital receipts, secure authentication, and activity logging . MIT licensed.

- **[Charitable](https://www.wpcharitable.com/)**  
  WordPress donation plugin with 0% platform fee and full data ownership. Native WordPress integration with recurring donation support. Best for organizations with self-hosted WordPress sites wanting complete control over donor data .

- **[Donation Platform for WooCommerce](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wc-donation-platform/)**  
  WordPress plugin enabling fundraising and donation management through WooCommerce. Supports recurring donations with WC Subscriptions, multiple products in cart, donor covers processing fees option, and Google Pay/Apple Pay/PayPal Express Checkout integration .

- **[Elrax](https://github.com/elrax/elrax)**  
  Open-source video crowdfunding platform powered by Cloudflare Workers and React Native, AGPL-3.0 licensed . Features video-based project pitches, advanced user profiles with donation history, development series for trust-building, and voting mechanisms for backers .

- **[GoFundFunding](https://github.com/gofunding/gofunding)**  
  Open-source crowdfunding platform .

- **[Donazy](https://github.com/kodingworks/donazy)**  
  Open-source donation and fundraising platform .

- **[Human Care Foundation](https://zenodo.org/records/19702237)**  
  Web-based NGO management platform with Django backend, SQLite database, and Bootstrap 5 frontend . Features donation processing, volunteer registration, help request handling, event coordination, contact management, and administrator authentication with clear privilege boundaries .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **awesome-donation-management** — Curated list of open-source donation management tools and resources.
- **NGO Management System** — PHP-based comprehensive NGO management with donor management, volunteer coordination, donation tracking, and reporting .
- **Aurorah CAN Digital Hub** — FastAPI-based community support and donor management platform for nonprofit outreach, volunteer coordination, donation tracking, and impact reporting .
- **Aisaar Charity Management Network** — Designed for administrative control, donation control, and transparency in charity operations .

**Frameworks for building custom donation management solutions**: Combine **CiviCRM** for comprehensive nonprofit CRM with donor lifecycle management, contributions, events, and memberships . Use **ChurchCRM** for church-specific member and contribution management with 40+ language support . Deploy **GiveWP** or **Charitable** for WordPress-based donation collection with 0% platform fees and full data ownership . Integrate **Sistema de Tesouraria** for church financial management with tithe tracking and MPESA integration . For video-based fundraising, **Elrax** provides a modern React Native foundation . Note that true enterprise donation platforms with managed payment processing, automated tax receipts, and compliance certifications remain primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong CRM, giving management, and financial tracking foundations that require integration for complete donation operations.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Donation management tools handle sensitive donor and financial data. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, PCI DSS compliance for card processing, and data privacy compliance (GDPR, CCPA).
- Payment processing integrations require merchant accounts and compliance with card network rules. Tax receipt generation varies by jurisdiction and requires legal review.
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong CRM, giving management, and financial tracking foundations, but managed payment processing, automated tax receipts, and enterprise compliance certifications remain primarily commercial offerings.
